# Code of conduct

Be respectful and helpful.

- Discuss ideas, not personal attacks.
- Welcome beginners and explain feedback clearly.
- Do not harass, threaten, or discriminate against anyone.
- Do not post private information or media without permission.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove harmful contributions and may block people who repeatedly ignore these rules.

Report serious conduct problems privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile contact options.
